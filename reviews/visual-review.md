# Visual review

One review round on Medium (1920x1080, Iris Xe), as agreed with the user to save budget. A separate agent saw only six renders of the viewpoints, and was told which things are deliberate choices: the empty square, the one puddle, the knee-deep water at Manga Hiti, and the mud houses.

## Fixed after the review

| Finding | Severity | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| Black gap between two palace wings (view 4) | minor | Mul Chowk was 2 m too narrow to meet Keshav Narayan Chowk; it now joins it. |
| Palace roof reads as flat | major | Steeper, deeper tile roof (3.4 m rise, 1.6 m overhang), now visible from the square. |
| Shikhara spires read as smooth cones | critical | Every storey (bhumi) of the tower now starts with a projecting ledge and a ribbed ring. |
| Warm brown shadows, flat sky, too much haze | major | Shade is lit by a cooler, bluer sky; the zenith is bluer; haze is reduced by a fifth. |
| Blocky shadow edges | major | Medium's sun shadow map is now 4096 (drawn once at load, so it costs memory, not frame time). |
| Puddle too mirror-perfect | major | A stronger constant ripple on all water breaks the reflection up a little. |
| Stone temples washed out in full sun | major | Stone darkened about 15% (done just before the review). |

## Not changed, and why

- **"Krishna Mandir is wrong" (views 1, 2, 6) and "wrong temple type in view 1":** the foreground temple in view 1 is the octagonal Krishna temple (Chyasim Deval), not Krishna Mandir. Krishna Mandir is in view 2, with its colonnades, kiosks and central tower. The reviewer mixed the two up. The ledges above make both towers read better.
- **"Garuda pillar has no cobra":** the cobra hood belongs to King Yoganarendra's pillar (the gilded figure in view 1), and it is there. The Garuda pillar's Garuda is stone, as it really is.
- **"No golden gate":** it is on Keshav Narayan Chowk, which is not in these six views.
- **Brick texture on the palace, more paving wear, chipped edges, carvings in Tusha Hiti:** these would be worthwhile, but each is a larger piece of work. Left for a later round if the user wants it.
- **Roof counts:** they follow REFERENCES.md (Char Narayan has two roofs, Hari Shankar three, Bhimsen three).

## The review as received

Critical: Krishna Mandir does not match the real temple (21 shikharas, ribbed spires); a wrong temple type in the view 1 foreground.
Major: the Garuda pillar figure is ambiguous; the palace front is flat with tiny windows and no golden gate; the palace roof reads as flat; the puddle is nearly mirror-perfect; one warm cast everywhere, with warm brown shadows and a flat sky; blocky shadow edges.
Minor: the paving is too regular and clean, with some far aliasing; the roof tiers have no visible tile rows; the edge houses are a little tidy; stone and brick look newly built; Manga Hiti looks like a clean tank; Sundari Chowk is bare; a few artifacts (a dark halo at the puddle edge, a gap at a palace wall seam).
Verdict: "A convincing mood and a good sense of scale, but the signature Krishna Mandir is not accurate. The palace is a flat, uniform slab without its carved brick and golden window character. The lighting is monochrome and flat, and the puddle is too clean and mirror-like. It is a decent stylized base, but it is not yet recognisable as Patan."
